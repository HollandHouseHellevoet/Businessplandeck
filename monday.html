II. Where the Money Goes

Each check lands somewhere.
None of it lands with you.

Float retained by carriers. Surplus accumulating on carrier balance sheets. Commission paid to brokers. Network access fees collected by PPO networks. Adjudication fees paid to ASOs. Reinsurance margins captured upstream. Every one of those dollars came from a patient encounter at your facility. None of them stay.

01 / Float
Carrier float
Premium paid up front. Claims paid months later. The capital in between does not belong to you.
02 / Surplus
Carrier surplus
When claims come in lower than expected, the unused premium accrues to someone else’s balance sheet.
03 / Commission
Broker commission
Pulled off the top of premium every renewal, whether the program performs or not.
04 / Network
Network access
Lease fees paid for the right to send your patients to a list of names.
05 / Adjudication
ASO adjudication
Per-employee fees paid to an outside platform for processing claims you funded.

Somewhere between 30 and 45 cents of every premium dollar never pays a claim. It accrues to a balance sheet you do not own.
